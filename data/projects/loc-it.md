LOC IT was created as a bonus deliverable for our challenge during the Henkel Philippines Hackathon 2026.

While our main campaign proposal focused on #LOCINWITHLOCTITE, LOC IT served as a practical demonstration of how the campaign could extend into a digital consumer experience—helping consumers discover the right LOCTITE product, understand why it fits their needs, and find a path toward purchase.

The prototype was developed within the hackathon timeframe as a concept demonstration, rather than as a production-ready commercial platform. Visit in https://github.com/rhbntm/loc-it to see more details
