For context, one of the projects that I've been working on: JR Inventory Management System is tied to this business that I'm helping https://linktr.ee/j.r.business.hq . The business is an online **LIVE** selling clothing shop based on Shopee which started operating around November 2026 so it's quite a new business <1 year old.

So now, I decided to increase my priority in helping my friend with his business because I'll be applying my skills as a software developer anyways, so I figured why not do it while actually solving real business problems. 

In fact, I was able to apply the same principle used in software development like the SDLC that we've learned **conceptually** in School but not as much in **procedurally** applying in real life with real business problems.

So for instance, my friend and I had to plan how to better operationalize our day to day lives in a way that will allow us to efficiently handle tasks while getting the most out of it. Welcome to leverage I guess? Then logically, we have to gather the variables that we need to which we will base our design of our day to day life. We're currently in this phase so we haven't tested it yet. But doesn't that sound familiar? Yeah SLDC applied in efficiently operationalizing the business.

Anyways there's a lot that I've procedurally(that is took actual action((instead of just thinking about concepts in my head)) learned today and I didn't even mention the technical ones like doing a grill-me session with my plan of adding a new feature in the J&R Inventory. 

Lots of opportunities and inevitable challenges ahead as well, but we'll move forward regardless. Welcome to cringe
