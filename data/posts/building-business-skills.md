I haven't been able to get intimate with programming or tech lately. I've been locked in on business stuff, so this is just a quick update and a short note on my tech-related plans.

I've basically become a host in my friend's business on top of being the one editing and posting daily videos, which is why I haven't had much time—or even the mindset—to fully immerse myself in tech recently.

That said, I've learned a lot from helping grow the business. My communication skills have improved quite a bit, and I've discovered that I'm capable of pushing myself much harder than I previously thought. It's been a good reminder that growth often happens outside of your comfort zone.

Interestingly, being involved in the day-to-day operations of a business has also given me a better appreciation for the problems software is meant to solve. Instead of only thinking about features and code, I've been exposed to challenges involving communication, workflows, inventory, marketing, and scaling. I think this perspective will help me build more practical software in the future.

Going forward, I'd like to set aside at least 30 minutes a day for tech-related learning. That could be watching YouTube videos, reading blog posts, exploring a random curiosity, or even discussing ideas with an LLM. More importantly, I want to immediately apply whatever I learn so the concepts stick and become part of my skill set.
